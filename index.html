Key insights for elite property investors.

What is the entry price point?

+

Our minimum investment threshold is AED 10M for off-market opportunities. This ensures access to institutional-grade properties with proven appreciation potential and exclusive developer allocations.

Do you offer relocation support?

+

Yes, we provide comprehensive relocation services including Golden Visa facilitation, school enrollment, banking relationships, and lifestyle integration for UHNW families moving to the UAE.

Who can access off-market listings?

+

Off-market access is reserved for qualified ultra-high-net-worth clients with established wealth management relationships and serious UAE investment intent. Pre-qualification is required.

How can I book a consultation?

+

Schedule a confidential consultation through our secure booking system. All discussions are NDA-protected and tailored to your specific investment objectives and timeline.

What are the tax implications for international buyers?

+

UAE property investments offer significant tax advantages including zero personal income tax, no capital gains tax, and favorable double taxation treaties with 140+ countries. We coordinate with your existing tax advisors to optimize your global tax position.

How does the Golden Visa program work?

+

Property investments of AED 2M+ qualify for the UAE Golden Visa, providing 10-year renewable residency for you and your family. We handle the entire application process and coordinate with immigration specialists.

What financing options are available?

+

UAE banks offer competitive mortgage rates for international buyers, typically 70-80% LTV for residents and 50-60% for non-residents. We have preferred relationships with private banks offering institutional-grade financing solutions.

How long does the purchase process take?

+

Ready properties can complete in 2-4 weeks with proper documentation. Off-plan investments follow developer payment schedules. We expedite all processes and coordinate with your legal and financial teams globally.

What ongoing management services do you provide?

+

Our institutional-grade asset management includes rental optimization, property maintenance, financial reporting, and exit strategy planning. We act as your single point of contact for all UAE property matters.

Can I purchase property through a corporate structure?

+

Yes, we facilitate purchases through UAE and international corporate structures for tax optimization and estate planning. Our legal partners specialize in cross-border structuring for UHNW families and family offices.
